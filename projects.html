My Process

How I approach technical projects

I use a repeatable process to move from an idea to a working, documented result.

01

Plan

Define the problem, goals, tools, requirements, and expected outcome.

02

Build

Create the project in manageable stages and test each part as it develops.

03

Verify

Confirm that the project works and collect evidence such as screenshots, terminal output, or test results.

04

Document

Explain the purpose, setup, technologies, challenges, results, and future improvements.
